        How it works

        1. We set up your team

          Your team gets its own address, like getbackpost.com/yourschool, with your colors and logo.

        2. You add your roster

          Create a login for each player and put them on a roster: Varsity, JV, or anything you call them.

        3. Players log their week

          They check in for Ranked Sessions and Training, track wins and losses, and write what to work on next.

        4. You coach from one screen

          See who's done, who's in session, and who's behind. Leave notes, review replays, and post the week's scrims.

        Weekly requirements

        Set how many Ranked Sessions and Training sessions count each week, per roster or per player. Past weeks keep their numbers.

        Scrims, matches and film

        One schedule for the whole program. Each event goes to the rosters it's for, and players mark if they're in.

        Replay reviews

        Players flag the game they want looked at, right from the session. Coaches get a notification and a queue.

        Ranks and trends

        Every player's rank and MMR with an eight-week trend, so progress is something you can point to.

        Free for schools

        Backpost is free for college, high school and club esports programs. It installs on phones like an app and sends notifications.
